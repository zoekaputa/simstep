Circular Motion Experiment

Welcome to the Circular Motion Experiment! In this simulation, you'll explore how objects move in circular paths. You'll see how speed, track inclination, and other factors affect the motion of a bike on a circular track. Pay attention to how the bike leans and how it stays on the track as you change different variables.
