Pendulum Experiment: Rhythms of Motion

Welcome to the Pendulum Experiment! In this interactive simulation, you'll explore the fascinating world of simple harmonic motion. By adjusting various properties of a pendulum, you'll discover how factors like length, mass, amplitude, and gravity affect its motion. Watch closely as the pendulum swings back and forth, and observe how energy transforms between potential and kinetic forms. Get ready to uncover the rhythmic patterns that govern the pendulum's dance!
