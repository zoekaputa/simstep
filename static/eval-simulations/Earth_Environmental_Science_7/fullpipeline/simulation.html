Atmospheric Layers Explorer

Welcome to the Atmospheric Layers Explorer! In this interactive simulation, you'll explore the different layers of Earth's atmosphere using a virtual skyscraper. Each floor represents a different atmospheric layer, from the ground-level troposphere to the space-bordering exosphere. As you move through the layers, observe how temperature, pressure, and composition change with altitude. Use the elevator control to navigate, and the measure and record buttons to collect data for analysis.
