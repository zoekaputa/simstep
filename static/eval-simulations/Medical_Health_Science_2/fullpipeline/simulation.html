Therapeutic Transport Network Simulation

Welcome to the Therapeutic Transport Network Simulation! In this experiment, you'll explore how drugs move through the human body and how different factors affect their distribution and effectiveness. You'll learn about pharmacokinetics, which is the study of how drugs are absorbed, distributed, metabolized, and eliminated by the body.

Use the controls below to adjust various properties of the drug and its delivery method. Observe how these changes affect the drug's movement and concentration in different parts of the body. Your goal is to develop strategies that maximize the drug's effectiveness while minimizing potential side effects.
