Pet Genetics Experiment

Welcome to the Pet Genetics Lab! In this experiment, you'll explore how traits are passed from parent pets to their offspring. You'll select parent pets, choose traits to study, and observe how these traits are inherited by the offspring. By using a digital Punnett square, you'll be able to predict and compare the actual results with theoretical probabilities. This experiment will help you understand the fascinating world of genetics and how traits are passed down through generations.
