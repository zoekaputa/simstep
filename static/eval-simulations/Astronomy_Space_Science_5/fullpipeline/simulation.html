Extreme Gravitational Phenomena: Black Hole Simulation

Welcome to the Black Hole Simulator! In this experiment, you'll explore the fascinating world of extreme gravitational environments. Observe how objects behave near a black hole and how the fabric of spacetime gets distorted. Adjust the black hole's properties and see how they affect the surrounding space. Place test particles and watch their trajectories as they approach the event horizon. Can you predict what will happen to them?
