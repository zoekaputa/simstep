Tire Pressure Experiment

Welcome to the Tire Pressure Experiment! In this simulation, you'll explore how temperature affects the pressure inside a tire. Imagine you're a scientist studying the behavior of gases in confined spaces. Your mission is to understand the relationship between temperature and pressure in a tire filled with air molecules.

Use the controls below to adjust the initial pressure and temperature. Then, watch how changes in temperature affect the tire's pressure over time. Pay close attention to how the air molecules behave and how the pressure gauge responds. Can you predict what will happen when you increase or decrease the temperature?
