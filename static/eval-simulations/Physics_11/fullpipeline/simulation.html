Water Slide Physics Simulation

Welcome to the Water Slide Physics Simulation! In this experiment, you'll explore how different factors affect the movement of water and a rider on a water slide. By adjusting various parameters, you can observe how slide design, water flow, and rider properties interact to create an exciting and safe water slide experience.

Use the controls below to modify the slide and run the simulation. Pay attention to how changes in height, water flow, and slide design impact the rider's speed, water pressure, and friction effects. Can you create the perfect water slide that's both thrilling and safe?
