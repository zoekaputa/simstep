Fruit Battery Experiment

Welcome to the Fruit Battery Experiment! In this interactive simulation, you'll explore how fruits can be used to generate electricity. You'll learn about electrochemical cells, redox reactions, and how different materials and configurations affect the electrical output. Get ready to power up an LED using nothing but fruit and metal strips!
