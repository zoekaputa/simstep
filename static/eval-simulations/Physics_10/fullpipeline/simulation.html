Dance Party Lighting System

Welcome to the Dance Party Lighting System! In this experiment, you'll explore how electricity powers different types of lighting circuits. You'll learn about series and parallel circuits, and how they affect the brightness of lights. Play around with the controls to see how changing the voltage, resistance, and circuit type impacts the flow of electricity and the overall illumination of your dance party!
