Playground Slide Friction Experiment

Welcome to the Playground Slide Friction Experiment! In this interactive simulation, you'll explore how different factors affect the way objects slide down a playground slide. You'll get to change the slide's material, angle, and even the weight of the test object. By observing how these changes impact the descent time and temperature, you'll discover some fascinating principles about friction and energy conversion. Get ready to slide into science!
